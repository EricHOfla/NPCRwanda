1. Executive Summary

This proposal recommends a complete frontend redesign of the National Paralympic Committee of Rwanda (NPC Rwanda) website using modern HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap.

The goal is to create a professional, accessible, and internationally competitive interface that:

  • Represents NPC Rwanda with the same standard as other National Paralympic Committees.
  • Better serves persons with disabilities, including visually impaired users, through strong accessibility practices.
  • Improves navigation, visual design, and content structure while keeping the implementation scope frontend only in this phase.

This phase focuses on UI/UX and responsive layout and will be built so it can later be connected to any backend or CMS.

2. Why This Frontend Project Is Important

2.1 Professional Image & International Standards

  • NPC Rwanda represents the country at continental and global level.
  • The current interface looks like a basic template: repeated content, inconsistent layout, and non-polished English.
  • A redesigned UI will bring us visually closer to the International Paralympic Committee and African Paralympic Committee standards.

2.2 Accessibility & Inclusion

  • As a Paralympic body, the website must be a practical example of inclusion.
  • The current frontend lacks full screen reader optimization, semantic structure, and ARIA labels.
  • The new frontend will be intentionally designed to better serve visually impaired and keyboard-only users, following WCAG principles.

2.3 Transparency & Trust

  • Donors, partners, and international federations expect easy access to governance structure, policies, annual and financial reports, and strategic plans.
  • The redesign will create clear, well-structured pages that present this information in a professional way, even if documents are simple PDFs at first.

3. Objectives of the Project (Frontend Scope)

  • Redesign the full visual interface using a modern, consistent design system.
  • Reorganize content to remove repetition and present information clearly.
  • Implement a responsive layout that works across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
  • Integrate accessibility best practices for visually impaired users.
  • Create a clear structure for key sections: Sports, Athletes, News, Governance & Transparency, Partners, Contact.
  • Ensure the frontend is clean, maintainable HTML/CSS/Bootstrap, ready for future backend/CMS integration.

4. Design System & UX Direction

4.1 Color Palette (Aligned with Logo)

  • Primary Blue: #0072C6 – stripe in the sun, main brand color
  • Accent Yellow: #FFD700 – the sun, key call-to-action highlight
  • Accent Green: #4CAF50 – ground/leaf, secondary banners and sections
  • Paralympic Red: #E53935 – small accents for alerts or emphasis
  • Background White: #FFFFFF – clean base that makes the logo stand out
  • Text Black: #000000 – primary text and headings for readability

4.2 Typography

  • Headings: Poppins or Montserrat – bold, modern, highly readable.
  • Body: Open Sans or Roboto – clean, international standard.
  • Buttons: Uppercase, semi-bold, clear calls to action.

4.3 Layout Principles

  • Max content width: 1200px container on desktop.
  • 8px spacing system with generous white space.
  • Rounded corners (8–12px) and soft shadows for cards.
  • Smooth hover animations on buttons, cards, and navigation.
  • Fully responsive using Bootstrap grid and utilities.

5. Page & Component Structure (Deliverables)

5.1 Homepage

The homepage will include:

  • Sticky Navigation Bar with logo, main sections (Home, About, Sports, Athletes, News, Governance, Contact) and a highlighted “Donate” button.
  • Hero Section (80vh) with a background image of Paralympic athletes, headline “Empowering Ability. Inspiring Rwanda.”, mission subtext, and buttons “Learn More” and “Become a Partner”.
  • About Preview with a two-column layout (image + short summary) and a “Read More” link to the About page.
  • Sports Programs grid with four cards for key disciplines such as Sitting Volleyball, Wheelchair Basketball, Amputee Football, and Para Athletics.
  • Featured Athletes section with athlete cards (photo, sport, achievements, “View Profile” button).
  • Latest News cards with image, date, title, summary, and “Read more” links.
  • Governance & Transparency Preview with icons/tiles for Board Members, Annual Reports, Policies, and Strategic Plan.
  • Partners logo strip with grayscale-to-color hover effect.
  • Call to Action full-width green band: “Support Inclusive Sports in Rwanda” with “Donate Now” and “Volunteer” buttons.
  • Footer with About, Quick Links, Governance, Contact and a small “Admin Login” link (instead of public webmail).

5.2 About Page

  • History of NPC Rwanda.
  • Vision & Mission.
  • Leadership (Board Members grid with roles and optional photos).
  • Strategic Objectives presented in a clear bullet or block format.

5.3 Athletes Page

  • Filter area (layout only in this phase): by sport, gender, achievement.
  • Athlete cards: photo, name, sport, classification, medal count.
  • Individual athlete profile template with biography, achievements timeline, photos/videos, and sponsors area.

5.4 News Page

  • Search bar layout.
  • Category filter (buttons or dropdown).
  • News listing cards with basic pagination UI.

5.5 Governance Page

  • Organizational structure layout or diagram.
  • Download blocks for Policies, Financial Reports, and Constitution (PDF).
  • Simple, document-style layout for easy reading.

5.6 Contact Page

  • Contact form (name, email, subject, message) – frontend only.
  • Map embed placeholder.
  • Office address, phone, email, and social media links.

6. Accessibility & UX Enhancements

  • Semantic HTML with proper headings, lists, and landmark elements (header, nav, main, footer).
  • Alt text for all meaningful images to support screen readers.
  • ARIA labels for buttons, navigation, and interactive elements where appropriate.
  • Full keyboard navigation support with visible focus states.
  • High color contrast between text and background, aligned with WCAG contrast ratios.
  • Consistent font sizes and line heights for readability.
  • Optional layout for a “Listen to this page” button for future text-to-speech integration.

7. Technology Stack (Frontend Only)

  • HTML5 for semantic structure and clean markup.
  • CSS3 for custom styling following the design system.
  • Bootstrap 5 for responsive grid, components, and utilities.
  • Optional minimal JavaScript (vanilla) for interactions such as menu toggles and simple sliders.

No backend framework (Laravel, WordPress, React, etc.) is required in this phase; however, the frontend will be built to integrate easily with a future backend or CMS.

8. Implementation Plan & Timeline

Total estimated duration: 6 weeks (adjustable based on priorities).

  • Week 1 – Discovery & Content Cleanup: review current content, remove duplication, define final page structure, approve sitemap and navigation.
  • Week 2 – UI Design & Wireframes: create static wireframes/mockups for key pages and approve colors, typography, and component style.
  • Weeks 3–4 – Frontend Development: build main pages in HTML/CSS/Bootstrap, implement navigation, hero, cards, grids, and responsive behavior.
  • Week 5 – Accessibility & Polish: add ARIA attributes, alt text placeholders, focus styles, check contrast and readability, refine spacing and hover states.
  • Week 6 – Review, Testing & Handover: cross-browser testing, content proofing, and handover of final code and basic documentation.

9. Risks & Mitigation

  • Content readiness: some governance or transparency documents may not be finalized.
    Mitigation: use placeholders and clear "Coming Soon" areas; the structure will be ready once documents are approved.
  • Image quality: lack of high-quality athlete photos.
    Mitigation: use temporary images until official media is available, then replace easily.
  • Future backend integration: backend decisions may come later.
    Mitigation: use clean, modular HTML/CSS so any backend (WordPress, Laravel, Headless CMS) can plug in later.
