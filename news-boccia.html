  1. Home

Dec 2025 · Program Update

The national boccia showcase in Kigali confirmed a growing talent pipeline and stronger district interest in precision para-sport pathways.

The event helped coaches and classifiers identify athletes with high potential while raising awareness among families and community leaders.

Strategic Priorities

  • Expand district-level talent identification and classification.
  • Increase coach development focused on precision sports.
  • Strengthen pathways to regional competition calendars.
"Boccia is vital for inclusion because it creates competitive pathways for athletes with severe physical impairments."

NPC Rwanda is working with partners to secure better equipment access and structured competition cycles for long-term athlete development.
