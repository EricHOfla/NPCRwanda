Goalball Championship Highlights Rwanda's Rising Talent

  1. Home

Feb 2026 · Competition Update

Rwanda's goalball program delivered one of its strongest championship performances, with improved tactical discipline and communication under pressure.

The event offered valuable competition exposure for blind and visually impaired athletes and demonstrated the impact of structured coaching cycles and regular camp preparation.

What Improved

  • Defensive organization and in-game communication.
  • Transition from ball recovery to attack.
  • Team confidence in high-pressure moments.
"Visibility and sustained investment in goalball can transform opportunities for visually impaired athletes nationwide."

NPC Rwanda is prioritizing district talent pathways, coaching support, and adaptive equipment access to sustain this momentum into upcoming regional competitions.
