Jan 2026 · International Friendly

The international friendly series offered a high-value platform for team preparation and tactical benchmarking before major continental fixtures.

Coaches used the series to validate combinations, improve first-ball control, and refine transition efficiency from defense to attack.

Performance Focus

  • Serve pressure and reception quality.
  • Block-to-defense coordination.
  • Leadership communication in final sets.
"Friendlies at this level build confidence and expose exactly where we must improve before qualifiers."

NPC Rwanda will maintain this match model to strengthen consistency and athlete readiness for future international competition.
