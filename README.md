## About NPC Rwanda

The **National Paralympic Committee of Rwanda (NPC Rwanda)** is the national body dedicated to the development and promotion of Paralympic sport in Rwanda. It supports and represents Rwandan athletes with disabilities and works to strengthen opportunities for participation, inclusion, and achievement in sport at national and international levels.

## About This Platform

This is NPC Rwanda's official digital platform. It serves as a central place for athletes, members, partners, supporters, and the public to learn about the Committee, follow its work, access official information, and engage with its activities.

The platform supports NPC Rwanda's communication, visibility, and public engagement. It is an organizational website and service—not an open-source software project.

## What You Can Find Here

- **About NPC Rwanda** — The Committee's background, mandate, leadership, and governance.
- **Athletes** — Information about Rwandan Paralympic athletes and their sporting journeys.
- **Sports and Activities** — Information about Paralympic sports and the Committee's work to develop them in Rwanda.
- **News and Announcements** — Updates, stories, achievements, and official communications.
- **Events** — Information about upcoming and past sporting events and activities.
- **Members and Partners** — Information about the organizations and people who contribute to the Paralympic movement in Rwanda.
- **Resources** — Official documents, policies, and useful information.
- **Opportunities** — Career and volunteer opportunities when available.
- **Get Involved** — Ways to support and contribute to the development of Paralympic sport in Rwanda, including donations.

## Our Purpose

Through this platform, NPC Rwanda aims to:

- Increase the visibility of athletes with disabilities and their achievements.
- Share accurate and timely information about Paralympic sport in Rwanda.
- Promote inclusion and equal opportunities through sport.
- Keep athletes, members, partners, and the public informed about the Committee's activities.
- Make important organizational information and resources easier to access.
- Encourage collaboration and support for the growth of Paralympic sport.
