Community Talent Days Expand Grassroots Reach

  1. Home

Nov 2025 · Grassroots Development

District outreach events connected new athletes and families to local para-sport programs, strengthening entry pathways from community to club level.

The Talent Days model combines awareness sessions, introductory sport activities, and referral support so families can access the nearest development centers.

Program Outcomes

  • Increased registration of first-time athletes.
  • Better district referral coordination.
  • Improved local visibility of inclusive sports opportunities.
"Grassroots systems are the foundation of elite success in Paralympic sport."

NPC Rwanda is scaling this outreach format to additional districts to ensure broader, earlier access to inclusive sport pathways.
